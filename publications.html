      Key indices

      What the main national indices measure, and where they come from. Series and base years change; always confirm on the official release.

      Consumer Price Index (CPI)

      Retail inflation. MoSPI announced a new series with base year 2024=100, scheduled from February 2026.

      Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

      Growth in mining, manufacturing and electricity. A new series with base 2022-23 was scheduled for May 2026.

      GDP / National Accounts

      Output and income of the economy. A new series with base year 2022-23 was scheduled for February 2026.

      PLFS bulletins

      Labour force participation, worker population ratio and unemployment. Monthly bulletins are scheduled for around the 15th, and quarterly bulletins each quarter.

      ASUSE

      Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises, with quarterly bulletins from MoSPI.

      HP: IIP, village directory, employee census

      The Himachal Pradesh Economics & Statistics Department runs its own Index of Industrial Production, Village Level Directory and Employee Census services.
